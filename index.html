LLM Pipeline Explorer

Type some text and watch it travel through a miniature LLM preprocessing pipeline: words become a vocabulary, tokens, token IDs, and finally vectors you can explore in 3D.

1

Unique words

each distinct word, in the order it first appears
2

Custom vocabulary & token IDs

every vocabulary token gets its own number

Peach tokens are special: <PAD> pads short sequences and <UNK> stands in for words the vocabulary has never seen.

3

Tokenization

text is split into tokens, then each token is swapped for its ID
4

Vector embeddings

each token becomes a list of numbers (first 6 of 8 shown)

Vectors are built from the words each token appears next to, so words used in similar contexts end up with similar numbers.

5

3D embedding map

closer points mean closer meaning

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, click a point to see its nearest neighbours.

6

Similarity heatmap

cosine similarity between every pair of tokens

Darker green means more similar. Hover a cell to read the pair.
